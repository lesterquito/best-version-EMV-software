Smart Card Reader

Omnikey readers (HID Global) are widely used contact and contactless smart card interfaces in EMV testing and laboratory environments. They provide reliable PC/SC connectivity for card personalization, application loading, transaction simulation, and formal evaluation of payment acceptance systems.

Deftun USB Reader/Writer

Compact USB smart card reader/writer commonly used for contact card operations. Supports reading and writing of EMV, Java Card and ISO 7816 compliant cards. Frequently employed in development, personalization and laboratory testing setups due to its simplicity and PC/SC compatibility.

JCOP Unfused Blank Cards

JCOP Unfused Blank Cards

Unfused JCOP (Java Card Open Platform) blank cards are essential for development, personalization and laboratory testing of EMV payment applications. These cards provide full control over the card operating system and application loading prior to final fusing, making them ideal for prototyping, formal evaluation and certification of payment acceptance systems.
