Smart Card Reader

Omnikey readers (HID Global) are widely used contact and contactless smart card interfaces in EMV testing and laboratory environments. Deftun USB Reader/Writer

Compact USB card reader/writer used for contact card operations. Supports reading and writing of EMV, Java Card and ISO 7816 compliant cards. Frequently employed in development, personalization and laboratory testing setups due to its simplicity and PC/SC compatibility.

JCOP Unfused Blank Cards

Unfused JCOP (Java Card Open Platform) blank cards are essential for development, personalization and laboratory testing of EMV payment applications. These cards provide control over the card operating system and application loading prior to final fusing.
